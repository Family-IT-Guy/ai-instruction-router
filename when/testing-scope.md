# What verification does this outcome need?

When choosing a check, name the consequential uncertainty and the observation that would settle it. Choose the method from the outcome and evidence. Do the work necessary to establish the agreed quality; a passing checklist or another model's agreement cannot supply missing judgment.

Use and observe a reversible internal result when use will reveal failure. Check before use when the mistake cannot be undone, the result reaches someone outside, or normal use would hide the failure. Preserve the user's approval of the actual public result, the two-turn send gate and production authorization.

Reuse evidence only when its provenance, relevant inputs, artifact revision, environment and coverage support the present claim. Changed relevant inputs reopen affected verification. Missing, stale or untrustworthy evidence stays unresolved. Integration needs its own evidence when component checks do not establish the outcome.

When a failure is repaired, reproduce the original failing case and inspect affected behavior. When a reviewer finding is disputed, check its basis against original evidence. Obtain another independent reader under `~/.agent/when/adversary-review.md` when the uncertainty requires one. A severity label does not determine whether a requirement is satisfied.

Record the plan as `Test plan: use and watch, or check before use because <which of the three>. Watch: <what, when, owner>. Undo: <how>.` Before release, state what will establish success in actual use, when it will be observed, who observes it, and how the change can be undone. Do not repeat an observation or add a suite merely because a prior round occurred. Repeat when changed inputs or unresolved uncertainty justify it. Do not test a variant that is not shipping. Do not build a harness that imitates an environment that could be used directly.
